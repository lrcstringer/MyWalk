Breaking Free

Freedom is a journey,
not a moment.

The Breaking Free programme pairs a dedicated practice with an accountability partner and a structured Recovery Path — a private journal that guides you through reflection, lapse tracking, and coping strategies. Serious support, built with grace.

  • Accountability partner — invite someone to walk the path alongside you
  • Freedom Journey journal — structured reflections separate from your main journal
  • Lapse recording — honest tracking without shame, building patterns for growth

AES-256 encryption

"Your journal is between
you and God."

Every journal entry and habit note is encrypted on your device before it reaches our servers. Not even we can read your private reflections.
