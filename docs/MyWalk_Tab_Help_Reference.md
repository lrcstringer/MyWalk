## 1. Today

**Your daily hub for gratitude, practice check-ins, and spiritual consistency.**

### What you can do

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| Daily Gratitude | Write one thing you're thankful for and share it with your circle. |
| Practice Check-ins | Tap each practice card when you've completed it for the day. |
| Week Navigation | Jump to any day in the current week to log past check-ins. |
| Mini-App Cards | Bible in a Year and Scripture Memorization cards are always visible — tap to open each programme. |

### Screen at a glance

| Element | What it does |
|---|---|
| Week strip | Tap a day to navigate |
| Daily Gratitude card (golden) | Write and submit today's gratitude |
| Bible in a Year card | Opens the Bible in a Year programme |
| Scripture Memorization card | Opens the Scripture Memorization programme |
| Practice cards | KINGDOM LIFE · PRACTICES sections — check in when done |

### How to use

1. **Open the app daily** — Today's date is selected automatically each time you open the app.
2. **Write your gratitude** — Tap the golden card, type what you're thankful for, then tap "Give thanks".
3. **Check off your practices** — Tap a practice card once you've completed it. It will animate to show it's done.
4. **Log a past day** — Tap any earlier day in the week strip to retroactively log check-ins.

## 2. Practices

**Discover and manage your daily spiritual disciplines.**

### What you can do here

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| Your Practices | See everything you are currently doing. Tap any practice to view or edit its details. |
| Add a Practice | Tap the "Add a Practice" button to browse disciplines by category — prayer, scripture, fasting, service, and more. |
| Breaking Free | Overcome challenges with a dedicated programme. Access via "Add a Practice". |
| Bible in a Year | A 52-week reading plan covering all 66 books of the Bible. |
| Scripture Memorization | Hide His word in your heart. Add verses and practise them daily. Premium feature. |

### How it works

1. **Discover a practice** — Tap "Add a Practice" to browse disciplines by category, or "Add a Mini-App" for structured programmes like Bible in a Year or Scripture Memorization.
2. **It appears in Today** — Once added, your practice shows up every day on the Today tab for you to check in.
3. **Manage from here** — Tap any practice in the Your Practices section to view details, edit, or stop the practice.

## 3. Journal

**Your private space for reflection, prayer notes, and spiritual insights.**

### What you can do

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| Write Entries | Capture reflections, prayers, or insights. Add text, photos, and voice notes. |
| Tag by Source | Link entries to a habit or a fruit of the Spirit for easy filtering later. |
| Journal Themes | Switch visual themes — parchment, night, and more — from the palette icon. |
| Search & Sort | Find any entry by keyword or sort by newest, oldest, habit, or fruit. |
| Freedom Journey | A second tab appears when you're on a Breaking Free path — tracks your recovery journal entries separately. |

### Entry card explained

| Element | Description |
|---|---|
| Date | Entry date |
| Pin icon | Pinned entries appear at top |
| Source chip | Habit name, fruit, or "Journal" |
| Media indicators | Photo 📷 or voice 🎤 |
| Text preview | Tap to read full entry |

### How to use

1. **Create a new entry** — Tap the pencil (✏️) button in the bottom-right corner to open the composer.
2. **Write your reflection** — Type freely. You can also attach a photo or record a voice memo.
3. **Tag your entry** — Choose a habit or fruit of the Spirit to categorise the entry for future filtering.
4. **Pin an important entry** — Long-press any entry card and choose "Pin" to keep it at the top of your list.
5. **Search your entries** — Tap the search bar at the top and type any keyword to filter results in real time.
6. **Change the theme** — Tap the palette 🎨 icon in the top bar to switch between journal visual themes.

## 5. Groups

**Pray together with family and friends. Spur one another on in love and good deeds.**

### What groups give you

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| Groups | Create private groups with family, friends, or accountability partners. |
| Shared Progress | See each member's check-in progress so you can pray for and encourage one another. |
| Sunday Summary | A weekly highlight reel of your circle's activity, delivered on Sundays. |
| Easy Invites | Share a simple invite code so anyone can join your group in seconds. |

### Group card explained

| Element | Description |
|---|---|
| Circle initial avatar | Coloured letter representing the group |
| ADMIN badge | Shown if you created the group |
| Member count | Number of people in the group |
| Chevron (›) | Tap to open the group |

### How to use

1. **Sign in to get started** — Tap "Sign in with Google" or "Sign in with Apple" on the Groups screen.
2. **Create or join a group** — Tap the + button and choose "Create a Group" or "Join with Code" to get connected.
3. **Invite others** — Open your group, find the invite code, and share it with friends or family.
4. **Share your gratitude** — After completing your daily gratitude on the Today screen, tap "Share with group."
5. **View your group's feed** — Tap any group card to see the shared feed, member progress, and prayer requests.
